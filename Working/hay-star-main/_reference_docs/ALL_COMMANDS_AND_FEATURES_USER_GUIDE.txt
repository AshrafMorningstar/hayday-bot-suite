 3. FIXES FOR THE PROBLEMS YOU REPORTED
================================================================================

PROBLEM 1: "AFTER COLLECTING FROM ANIMALS, THE ANIMALS GOT HIDDEN"
   - Why it happened:
     In Hay Day, when you harvest milk, eggs, or bacon, the animals enter an
     exhausted/starving state and collapse flat on the ground. When lying down,
     they look like they disappeared or are hiding!
   - How we fixed it:
     We updated `collect_animals` so that IMMEDIATELY after harvesting products,
     it automatically sends their exact feed. When fed, the animals jump up,
     smile, peck/chew happily, and remain 100% standing and visible on your screen!

PROBLEM 2: "THE ADVERTISEMENT TIMER WAS STUCK AT 5 MINUTES"
   - Why it happened:
     From the game configuration (`RoadSideShopStandAdvertisementCooldownMinutes, 5`),
     Supercell's game server enforces a strict 5-minute wait between free newspaper
     advertisements. If a bot tries to put an ad on every single crate at once,
     the game server rejects it or prompts for diamonds, making the timer look stuck.
   - How we fixed it:
     We built an intelligent Advertisement Cooldown Tracker (`ad_status`):
     - When you list items, it checks if 5 minutes have passed.
     - If YES: It attaches the free newspaper ad!
     - If NO: It lists your items cleanly without an ad so the crate posts instantly
       without freezing your screen or asking for diamonds!
     - You can check the countdown anytime with `ad_status` or in the GUI.

 5. NEW FEATURE: ROADSIDE SHOP AUTO-SELLING & ANTI-BAN PRICING
================================================================================

Sell any crop, good, or tool in your roadside shop crates automatically!

COMMAND:
  shop_sell [item_id] [slot] [count] [price_mode] [ad]

PARAMETERS:
  1. item_id    : The ID of the item to sell (default 400001 for Wheat).
  2. slot       : Which crate to put it in: `0`, `1`, `2`... or `all` for all crates.
  3. count      : How many items to put in the crate (default 10, max 10).
  4. price_mode : The pricing strategy:
                  * antibank -> (RECOMMENDED!) Random human-like price slightly
                                below maximum (e.g. 34 or 35 coins for 10 wheat
                                instead of static 36). Looks completely natural
                                to anti-bot algorithms!
                  * max      -> Full 100% maximum allowed game price.
                  * medium   -> Half price (~50%) for faster buying.
                  * low      -> 1 coin dump price (to empty barn/silo instantly).
                  * <number> -> Any custom price (e.g. 25).
  5. ad         : `auto` (uses free newspaper ad if 5-min cooldown is ready),
                  `0` (no ad), or `1` (force ad).

EXAMPLES (COPY & PASTE):
  1. Sell 10 wheat in Slot #0 with safe anti-ban pricing:
       shop_sell 400001 0 10 antibank auto

  2. Fill ALL shop crates with wheat at safe anti-ban price:
       shop_sell 400001 all 10 antibank auto

  3. Sell 10 corn at maximum price in Slot #1:
       shop_sell 400002 1 10 max auto

  4. Dump 10 wheat for 1 coin to clear space:
       shop_sell 400001 0 10 low 0
