In Google Antigravity, you can access and export your chat history in a couple of ways depending on whether you want to do it via the UI or directly from your local filesystem.

---

### Method 1: Via the UI (Chat Canvas / Sidebar)

1. **Chat Panel Options Menu**:
   - Open the conversation you want to export.
   - Look at the **top right** of the chat canvas header for the **`...` (More Options)** or **Export / Share** icon.
   - Click it to select **Export** (commonly formatted as Markdown `.md` or JSON).

2. **Left-Hand Conversation List**:
   - In the left sidebar under your chat history, hover over or right-click the conversation you wish to export.
   - Select **Export** or **Save Transcript**.

---

### Method 2: Accessing the Local Saved Transcripts Directly (Disk)

Antigravity automatically persists complete transcripts of every conversation to your local machine in JSON Lines (`.jsonl`) format.

#### File Locations:
On Windows:
```plaintext
C:\Users\<Username>\.gemini\antigravity-ide\brain\<conversation-id>\.system_generated\logs\
```
*(On macOS / Linux: `~/.gemini/antigravity-ide/brain/<conversation-id>/.system_generated/logs/`)*

Inside this folder, you will find:
- **`transcript_full.jsonl`**: The complete, untruncated chronological record of the conversation, including all user inputs, agent replies, tool calls, and outputs.
- **`transcript.jsonl`**: A compact, token-efficient version of the transcript.
- **Generated Artifacts**: Any plans, walkthroughs, or notes created in that session are stored in the parent folder: `...\brain\<conversation-id>\`.

---

### Tip: Referencing Past Chats in Antigravity
If your goal is to bring context from an earlier conversation into your current session, you don't need to manually export and re-paste it:
- Type **`@`** in the chat input box.
- Select **Conversations** from the mention menu to attach a previous chat session directly into your prompt.
